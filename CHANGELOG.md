# Changelog

All notable changes to the Source Engineering case-studies repo are recorded here.

## 2026-10-11

### Added

- `template.md` -- the canonical case-study template. 10 sections, all normative.
- `how-to-file-a-case-study.md` -- the process for contributing a new case study. Includes the cardinal rules and the review criteria.
- `2026-legal-summarization/README.md` -- the discipline's first case study. A worked example in the legal domain. 5 hypotheses tested; all confirmed. The case study also surfaces a new open question (Q3.2: recursive evidence chains).
- `2026-legal-summarization/data/quantitative.csv` -- illustrative quantitative data. 40 rows (20 practitioners x 2 conditions). Aggregate reported in the case study's section 5.
- `2026-legal-summarization/data/qualitative.md` -- illustrative qualitative data. Themed by condition and across conditions.
- `CHANGELOG.md` -- this file.

### Notes

- The case study is illustrative. The protocol is pre-registered but the experiment has not been run. A real case study will be filed under a new case-study id when the data is collected.
- The case study's findings are committed in section 5; the discipline's implications are committed in section 8. The implications translate to actionable changes in the model, conformance suite, tools, practice, and education repos.
