# Qualitative data for the 2026 finance transaction-routing case study

> Illustrative data. The protocol is pre-registered but the experiment has not been run. The themes below are a worked example of the case-study surface, coded against the same theme frame as the legal study.

## Themes from the COTS-on condition

### Catching the wrong route before execution

> "The misrouted probe was obvious once I saw the constraint it violated in the COTS. Without the COTS I would have executed it and caught it in reconciliation."

### The COTS as an execution gate

> "I treat the COTS like a pre-flight checklist. The context parameters and the evidence chain tell me whether the routing is defensible if compliance asks."

### When the COTS did not help

> "When the evidence chain resolved to the model's own scoring rather than the routing rules, I learned nothing new. Same finding as legal: recursive evidence does not close the hesitation factor."

## Themes from the COTS-off condition

### Reconstructing the rationale after the fact

> "The system said 'domestic-fast' and nothing else. I had to reverse-engineer why, and by then the window had passed."

### Playing it safe

> "I rejected routings I could not justify. That is slower, but a wrong wire is worse than a slow wire."

## Cross-condition themes

### Hesitation is about verifiability, not speed

> "In both conditions the analysts said the same thing: what I need is to see why, in time to act. The COTS is the only surface that gave them that."
